    Navbar

    The bar above is the specimen: .logo-link carrying both lockup images, .nav-divider, .brand (the page h1 plus its subtitle), .controls (a range with a live output, a labelled select, a three-way .seg-btns group, and the county combobox), then .nav-meta with the search toggle, two labelled buttons, the theme toggle and the round .sfsa-btn-info. Narrow the window to walk the ladder: 1400 clips the button and control labels (clipped, never display:none — that removes the accessible name), 1060 tightens the gaps, 750 swaps the wide banner for the square badge and hides the text lockup visually, 640 drops the refresh dot and collapses search into the magnifier.

    Panel and scrim

    A floating glass .sfsa-panel with a collapsible body, wired by core's initCollapsible: the toggle carries aria-expanded, the body animates shut and only then takes [hidden], so collapsed content leaves the tab order. It remembers its state under an app-prefixed key and starts collapsed on a compact viewport unless you have told it otherwise. In an app this is the legend dock over the map; the stage below stands in for that.

    The kit's other page-level surface is .sfsa-scrim — the dimming layer an app puts behind an off-canvas drawer on a compact viewport. It sits one tier below the drawer and, like every floating surface here, takes its number from the one z-index ladder rather than inventing one. Raise it and the detail card stays above it, because --z-detail outranks --z-map-panel.

    Toast

    Transient status, 2800 ms by default, one per page, announced politely through role="status". Core creates and appends the element on first use; the navbar's lightning button fires the same one. While a bottom sheet is docked the toast lifts above it by --sheet-h.

    Tooltip

    The pointer-following .sfsa-tooltip, at rest. It is aria-hidden decoration — a screen reader can no more follow a cursor than it can read a WebGL canvas, so the same content has to reach assistive tech another way (the live region and the card below the map do that here). The live one is on the county map at the bottom of this page.

    Detail card

    The docked detail surface (ui/card.js): role="dialog" with aria-modal="false" — deliberately not a <dialog>, because showModal() would make the map inert and panning, zooming and searching with a county open are exactly the things you do with a county open. Focus moves in on open and returns to the opener on close; Escape closes it, but yields to any layer above it. The card lives over the live map at the foot of this page, which is where an app puts it — and below 640px (or on a short landscape window) it stops floating and docks as a bottom sheet, stamping its height on --sheet-h so the map's corner controls and the toast lift clear of it.

    Legends

    Both legend forms from ui/legend.js. Colour is never the sole channel: the swatches are aria-hidden decoration and the labels, ticks and plain-language key carry the meaning. "No data" is a category, not an absence, and its chip is outlined so it survives the high-contrast theme. Ramps are data, so they are literal colour strings the caller owns — the kit neither tokenizes nor theme-swaps them.

    Search combobox

    The ARIA 1.2 combobox in the navbar (ui/search.js) is the keyboard twin for clicking a county on the canvas, which makes it an accessibility obligation rather than a convenience. Matching folds diacritics and case and ranks label-prefix over code-prefix over word-start over substring, results are capped with a counted overflow row, and every filter pass announces its total through the live region. Arrow keys move aria-selected while DOM focus stays in the input; Enter takes the active row, or the top hit if you never moved. It starts over a thirty-row fixture and is refreshed with the real county list once the map's geometry lands.

    Modal

    A native <dialog class="sfsa-modal"> filled by ui/help.js from demo/help-demo.md — repo-authored markdown, rendered with the vendored marked build and GFM tables on. Backdrop click closes, Escape closes, focus returns to whichever button opened it. No firstVisitKey here on purpose: a gallery that opens a modal at you on every visit is a gallery nobody reads.

    Live county map

    The whole map stack: createCompositeMap on a cream canvas with no basemap, no rotation and no pitch; house navigation and fit-to-extent controls fused into one group; the zoom floor that springs the camera back when the composite stops filling the frame; the FSA county composite decoded from the boundary archive for the program year's vintage; the pointer tooltip; and the detail card on click. County ids are 5-character FSA strings end to end — never FIPS, never parsed to a number. Export writes a branded PNG through ui/export.js.
